CreditCrate · Guide for testers

Everything you do outside your DAW.

You already make the music. CreditCrate helps you get it to the people who need to hear it. This guide walks you through the five tools, in the order you'll probably use them.

Before you start

Marius creates your account and sends you the email and password. Sign in at creditcrate.app. Sign-up is closed, so if you forget your password, ask Marius to reset it.

The five tools

ToolWhat it does
CreditsSearch an album and see the producers, engineers and mixers behind it, with the public emails we can find.
ContactsYour own list of people to reach. Add them by hand or import a CSV file.
PacksYour sample and beat packs, saved as Dropbox or Google Drive links.
CampaignsWrite an email, choose who receives it, and send it from your own Gmail.
Deal DeskSplit sheets, producer agreements and invoices. Everything stays in your browser.

The start page shows all five apps side by side. Swipe, drag or use the tabs at the top to move between them. Each app scrolls inside its own window.

Step by step

1 · Add your contacts

Open Contacts and press + to add one person. Or press + and then Import a CSV.

CSV format

The first row holds the column names. Only email is required.

email,name,instagram,roles,tags
alex@example.com,Alex Rivera,alexrivera,producer,trap
sam@example.com,Sam Lee,,mixer,drill

Anyone who unsubscribes is hidden from your list and never emailed again, even if you import them again.

2 · Save your packs

Upload your pack to Dropbox or Google Drive, then copy its share link. In Packs, press +, give it a name and paste the link.

Make sure the link is set to “anyone with the link can view”. Otherwise people who receive your email can't open it.

3 · Connect your Gmail

Campaigns send from your own Gmail address, so the emails land in your Sent folder. Connect once, in the Campaigns app, with an app password:

01

In your Google account, turn on 2-Step Verification.

02

Open App passwords in the same security settings, create one called CreditCrate, and copy the 16-character code.

03

In CreditCrate, enter your Gmail address and paste the code into Connect Gmail. We store it encrypted.

You can disconnect at any time with the same card.

Step by step

4 · Send a campaign

01

In Campaigns, write a subject and a message. Use {{name}} to put in each person's name. Choose a pack if you want to attach one.

02

Press Save draft. Then choose it in the Send card and press Preview recipients. Check who it would reach.

03

Press Send test to me and look at the email in your own inbox.

04

Press Send to … people and confirm. Emails go out one by one, a few seconds apart. The progress updates in the Send card.

Rules that keep your emails out of spam

Only write to people you have a real reason to contact, such as producers and engineers whose work you know.

CreditCrate allows 300 emails in any 24 hours. The Gmail card shows how many you have left.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Respect it, and leave those people out of future campaigns.

Keep messages short and personal. A short note with a link works better than a long pitch.

5 · See who opens your emails

The top of Campaigns shows who recently opened your email and your three top openers. Opens are estimates: some mail apps load images automatically, so they can count opens nobody read. Clicks are reliable.

Credits and Deal Desk

Credits: search an album and press it to see its credits. Every album you search shows up under Recent searches, with the emails found. Not every person has a public email, so treat the list as a starting point.

Deal Desk keeps everything in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, so export or copy your work before you clear your browser data.
