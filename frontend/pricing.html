Questions

What happens after 7 days?

The tools lock until you choose Pro. Your contacts, packs and campaigns are kept, so nothing is lost.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. You only pay if you choose Pro.

Do I need my own Gmail?

Yes. Campaigns and the sample inbox use your own Gmail account, connected with an app password, so mail goes out from your address.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars; sales tax or VAT may be added at checkout where it applies. Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel. Terms and refund rules are still to be written.
